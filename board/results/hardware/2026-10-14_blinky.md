# blinky on the Tang Nano 20K, 2026-10-14

Bitstream: board/build/blinky.fs, loaded with `make prog-blinky` (openFPGALoader 1.1.1, JTAG at 6 MHz; `--detect` reported a Gowin GW2A(R)-18(C)).

Observed by eye (by the board's owner), not logged by a program:
- the six LEDs counted up in binary, about 6 counts a second;
- holding S1 lit exactly one LED (LED 0).

What that shows (see board/blinky.v): the bitstream loads, the 27 MHz clock runs, the LEDs are active low as board/README.md says, and S1 reads 1 while pressed.
